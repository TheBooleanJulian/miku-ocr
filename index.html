What she's good at

Built for the images people actually send

Photos, pastes, or forwards

Send a photo directly, paste one from your clipboard, or forward it from another chat — she reads all three the same way.

Uncompressed files too

Images sent as Telegram "documents" work just as well as regular photo uploads — no quality gets stripped first.

Tap-and-hold to copy

Transcripts come back in a monospace block, formatted for a single copy — straight into a note, a form, or a message.

Cheap to run at volume

Images are downscaled before upload and every read runs on Claude Haiku 4.5 — accurate transcription without the API bill.

Nothing gets truncated

Long transcripts are split across multiple messages automatically, so a dense page of text still arrives in full.

No setup, no waiting

There's no account to create — message the bot and the first reply is your first transcription.

Under the hood

Four steps, no typing

Send

Drop in a photo, a clipboard paste, or a forwarded image with English text somewhere in it.

Downscale

The image is resized to Claude's useful resolution ceiling and re-encoded as JPEG — the single biggest lever on cost, with no real accuracy loss.

Read

Claude Haiku 4.5 transcribes every line of English it can find, ignoring logos, watermarks, and decorative noise.

Copy

The transcript comes back as a monospace block. Tap and hold, copy, paste it wherever it needed to go.
